Selected Systems.

Engineering solutions to real civic, transit, and environmental infrastructure challenges. Below are four of my most significant releases and research investigations.

01 / FLEET MOBILITY & CLOUD TELEMETRY

Jatra Transit.

Daily commuting across urban Dhaka is fraught with erratic schedule opacity and prolonged congestion. To solve this for East West University and public transit commuters, I designed and engineered Jatra — a comprehensive fleet tracking ecosystem.

Powered by sub-second driver GPS broadcast synchronization via Firebase Realtime Database, an interactive Leaflet.js dispatch dashboard, and a native Android application. Backed by an extensive 100-page engineering capstone spec analyzing geospatial failovers, geofenced route boundaries, and passenger load analytics.

02 / IOT & BUILDING AUTOMATION

SmartHome IoT.

Bridging live physical electronics with high-density architectural simulation. I engineered a centralized energy telemetry platform monitoring real Tuya smart plug hardware while modeling a simulated 10-floor, 40-room institutional digital twin.

Constructed on a hybrid backend pairing Python (Flask) for high-speed analytical number-crunching with Node.js for instantaneous WebSocket state distribution, providing real-time load-shedding safeguards and historical consumption curves.

03 / EMPIRICAL QUALITATIVE RESEARCH

Circularity Research.

A foundational academic investigation: "From Household Storage to Scrap Sorting: Qualitative Evidence on E-Waste Pathways in Bangladesh".

Investigating the domestic hibernation of obsolete smartphones and PCs, informal scrap dealer (*bhangari*) supply chains, and toxic heavy metal hazards in urban Dhaka. The paper formulates engineering recommendations for reverse-logistics software systems.

04 / MOBILE & CIVIC SYSTEMS

AttendanceCloud.

Developed for East West University under course CSE489 (Mobile Application Development), AttendanceCloud replaces fragile paper attendance sheets with an offline-first Android application pairing local SQLite persistence with seamless cloud synchronization.

Complemented by civic technology initiatives including the Yousuf Ali Munshi Bari Foundation Portal (PHP/MySQL) — delivering total financial transparency and monthly contribution tracking for community welfare in Cumilla.
